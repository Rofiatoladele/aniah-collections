Jogger size guide

Waist is the most important measurement. Use your height to check the length.

How to measure

  1. Waist: wrap a soft tape around the narrowest part of your waist, over thin clothing, and keep it snug but not tight.
  2. Height: stand straight against a wall without shoes and measure from the floor to the top of your head.
  3. Choose your size by waist first. If you are between two sizes, pick the bigger one for a relaxed fit.
